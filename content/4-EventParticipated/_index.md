During the internship, I attended 3 events. Each one gave me new knowledge and perspectives on AWS, Cloud and AI, along with the chance to meet and connect with fellow learners and industry professionals.

### [Event 1](4.1-event1/)

&emsp;**Event name:** Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global

&emsp;**Date & time:** 09:00 (UTC+7), 26/09/2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Content and main activities:** The kickoff of the FCAJ 2026 Buildrathon, organized together with CMC Global. The organizers introduced the learning path: a mandatory AWS Foundation track and 5 specialized tracks (DevOps & Automation, Security & Compliance, Data & Analytics, Serverless & Modern Applications, AI/ML & Generative AI), along with what each track requires from participants.

&emsp;**Outcomes:** A clear picture of the learning path for the internship and a direction for my team: build a solid AWS foundation first, then go deep into Serverless & Modern Applications combined with AI for our Serverless E-commerce project.

### [Event 2](4.2-event2/)

&emsp;**Event name:** AWS Cloud Day and AI Day Hanoi - Watch Party

&emsp;**Date & time:** 09:00 (UTC+7), 29/09/2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Content and main activities:** A hybrid Watch Party in Ho Chi Minh City that streamed AWS Cloud Day and AI Day, held in Hanoi. It included keynotes from leaders of large companies on how Agentic AI creates real business impact, and Builders/AI sessions where AWS Solutions Architects and engineers shared how they design data flows, optimize databases and connect APIs.

&emsp;**Outcomes:** I learned to look at a problem from two sides: the business side (which metrics to track, what value is actually delivered) and the technical side (architecture and data flow design), and I strengthened my habit of self-learning and asking questions to understand the root of a problem.

### [Event 3](4.3-event3/)

&emsp;**Event name:** Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner

&emsp;**Date & time:** 09:00 (UTC+7), 02/10/2026

&emsp;**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

&emsp;**Role:** Attendee

&emsp;**Content and main activities:** A community session with Dr. Werner Vogels, Chief Technology Officer of Amazon and one of the pioneers of cloud computing. It covered the future of Cloud and AI, lessons from building and operating systems at global scale, innovation and technology trends, followed by Q&A and networking with the community.

&emsp;**Outcomes:** A better understanding of how to think about systems at scale: accept that failures will happen and design the system to recover on its own, plus more motivation to pursue Cloud and AI.
