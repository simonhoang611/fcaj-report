# Event Report: "AWS Cloud Day and AI Day Hanoi - Watch Party"

### Event Information

- **Date & time:** 09:00 (UTC+7), 29/09/2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee

### Event Objectives

- Let learners in Ho Chi Minh City follow AWS Cloud Day and AI Day Hanoi live without traveling.
- Catch up on Cloud and AI trends, especially Agentic AI, from business leaders and AWS experts.
- Learn methods and problem-solving thinking rather than theory alone.

### Content and Main Activities

A hybrid Watch Party in Ho Chi Minh City that streamed AWS Cloud Day and AI Day, held in Hanoi. It included keynotes from leaders of large companies on how Agentic AI creates real business impact, and Builders/AI sessions where AWS Solutions Architects and engineers shared how they design data flows, optimize databases and connect APIs.

#### Keynotes: AI that creates business value

- Leaders of large companies shared how they put Agentic AI systems into operation and scale them.
- The key point was which metrics they track to measure the real impact of AI on the business.

#### Builders / AI track: breaking down the architecture

- AWS Solutions Architects and engineers shared how they design data flows, optimize database systems, set up platforms and connect APIs.
- Attendees could bring their own architecture questions to the experts.

#### The Watch Party format

- A live stream from Hanoi watched together with the learner community in Ho Chi Minh City, with time to talk and connect with others interested in Cloud and AI.

### Key Takeaways

- Structured thinking: break a big problem into parts that can be solved and measured.
- A technical solution only has value when it is tied to a concrete business metric.
- How AWS engineers look at a system through its data flow, database and APIs.

### Applying to Work

- For the AI product recommendation feature, the team needs a clear success metric (for example, how often shoppers add a recommended item to the cart) rather than only making the feature work.
- When designing DynamoDB tables and APIs, I start from the data flow and the queries that must run, the same approach shared in the Builders track.

### Lessons Learned

- Events are for learning how to think and solve problems, not for being shown every step.
- Self-learning and asking questions proactively are the most important skills in tech.
