# Event Report: "Buildrathon Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global"

### Event Information

- **Date & time:** 09:00 (UTC+7), 26/09/2026
- **Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, 2 Hai Trieu Street, Saigon Ward, Ho Chi Minh City
- **Role:** Attendee

### Event Objectives

- Officially kick off the FCAJ 2026 Buildrathon together with CMC Global.
- Introduce the learning path and the knowledge tracks participants follow during the internship.
- Help teams choose a direction and the skills to prepare before they start building.

### Content and Main Activities

The kickoff of the FCAJ 2026 Buildrathon, organized together with CMC Global. The organizers introduced the learning path: a mandatory AWS Foundation track and 5 specialized tracks (DevOps & Automation, Security & Compliance, Data & Analytics, Serverless & Modern Applications, AI/ML & Generative AI), along with what each track requires from participants.

#### Mandatory track: AWS Foundation

- Set up and manage a first AWS account, and control costs with AWS Budgets.
- Core architecture with VPC, EC2, S3 and RDS; identity and access management with IAM.
- Monitoring with CloudWatch; the AWS CLI and basic Infrastructure as Code.

#### 5 specialized tracks

- **DevOps & Automation:** CI/CD with CodePipeline/CodeBuild/CodeDeploy, advanced IaC with CloudFormation/CDK, containers with ECS/EKS, Blue/Green and Canary deployments.
- **Security & Compliance:** Zero Trust, identity federation and SSO, encryption and key management, security automation, compliance frameworks.
- **Data & Analytics:** data lakes, ETL with Glue, real-time analytics, MLOps, Business Intelligence, data governance.
- **Serverless & Modern Applications:** event-driven architecture, microservices, GraphQL APIs with AppSync, serverless patterns, application modernization, performance optimization.
- **AI/ML & Generative AI:** foundation models with Amazon Bedrock, RAG, LLM fine-tuning, AI-powered applications, multi-modal systems, MLOps.

#### Requirements for participants

- Each track sets requirements that participants must complete; the AWS Foundation track is mandatory for everyone.

### Key Takeaways

- A full view of the AWS knowledge areas and how they build on each other, from foundation to specialization.
- Why the foundation (account, cost, networking, IAM, monitoring) must be solid before building a real product.
- How to pick tracks based on the team's problem instead of learning everything at once.

### Applying to Work

- In the first weeks I focused on finishing the AWS Foundation track through the labs, including setting up AWS Budgets from day one to keep costs under control.
- My team chose the Serverless & Modern Applications direction (Lambda, DynamoDB, SQS/SNS, event-driven architecture) combined with AI product recommendations for our Serverless E-commerce project.
- We applied IaC with CDK and CI/CD from the DevOps track to deploy the project's infrastructure.

### Lessons Learned

- A clear roadmap makes learning goal-driven and lets me measure my own progress.
- A solid foundation is what lets you move fast in the specialized parts later.
